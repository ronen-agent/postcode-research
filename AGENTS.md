# Guidance for PostCode Research Agents

## Orientation

Read [README.md](README.md) for the project overview, status links, and document guide, then read the documents relevant to the task. Preserve the research scope, terminology, and distinctions established in those documents. Consult [methodology.md](methodology.md#8-evidence-and-claim-scope) when assessing evidence or making research claims, and follow the review conventions in [related-work.md](related-work.md) when updating the bibliography or comparisons.

For research reviews, follow [maintenance/research-watch.md](maintenance/research-watch.md).

When publishing automated proposals through GitHub, follow [maintenance/github-workflow.md](maintenance/github-workflow.md).

Material under `application/` describes foundation material for adoption in the application repository. Do not apply its implementation task-record protocol to ordinary research-document edits or automatically propagate changes to the application repository.

## Editing and review

Inspect the working tree before editing and preserve unrelated changes. Follow the human's instructions for commits and publication.

Preserve each document's role and established terminology. When changing a substantive claim, inspect linked discussions and update affected documents within the authorized scope; flag wider implications that need a separate decision. Keep edits focused and preserve existing review qualifications.

Check that substantive changes to research questions, commitments, or claims are explicit and supported by the stated rationale or evidence. Verify Markdown formatting and relative links. Report what was checked and any remaining uncertainty.
