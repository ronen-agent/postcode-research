## Working title

**PostCode: Design Knowledge for Architectural Practice Without Proficiency in the Implementation Language**

This working title assumes that unfamiliar-language formative work supports a bounded claim that consequential architectural practice is possible without proficiency in the implementation language. Otherwise, narrow it to **PostCode: Design Knowledge for Architectural Practice Without Exercising Proficiency in the Implementation Language**.

Alternative titles should follow the contribution actually supported rather than promising comparative superiority in advance.

## Abstract scaffold

Software-development agents make it increasingly possible for humans to direct implementation without reading or writing programming-language source, but humans still need representations through which to understand, design, and evaluate the resulting software. Capable general-purpose agents already explain repositories, propose designs, generate diagrams, and retain notes; the unresolved problem is what specialized, qualified, and retained architectural investigation contributes beyond that baseline.

We present PostCode, a tool for source-independent architectural investigation that provides partial, epistemologically qualified accounts of implemented, hypothetical, and intended architecture. We report a constructive design inquiry combining implementation of the artifact with sustained formative use during real software work, reconstructable research episodes, independent checks of consequential architectural claims, and explicit analysis of interventions and limitations.
