## Findings and next investigations

The proposed [bibliography changes](../../related-work.md) contain the detailed comparisons and qualifications.

### 1. Elicitation and evaluation validity can dominate pipeline complexity

Goel et al., [arXiv:2610.37830](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37830), submitted **27 October 2026**. Inspected primary metadata and selected full-text sections on study design, instrument validation, elicitation, pipeline ablations, results, discussion, and limitations.

**Implication:** in the studied smart-contract setting, exact replay and known-error controls overturn conclusions supported by LLM judges and looser scoring. Information elicited before generation matters far more than later review or repair, while verification disagreements are often better treated as questions than automatic corrections. This gives PostCode a concrete evaluation pattern for directing software: measure ambiguity surfaced, human response burden, omissions, exact behavioral evidence, retained intent, and downstream structure separately. The study uses a simulated client derived from an existing implementation and therefore measures optimistic implementation fidelity, not human author intent.

### 2. Structural specifications can constrain agents beyond behavioral examples

Golatkar et al., [arXiv:2610.38214](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38214), submitted **28 October 2026**. Inspected primary metadata and selected full-text sections on the control model, specification variants, benchmarks, results, related work, and artifact definitions.

**Implication:** Skeleton Coding provides close prior art for architecture as an active specification, reporting lower test error and repair effort when modules, types, interfaces, and signatures are fixed alongside behavioral requirements. Its skeletons are programming-language source and primarily agent-facing, so they do not answer PostCode's human-facing, no-source question. They do establish a strong comparison condition: express equivalent structural commitments through skeletons, PostCode representations, and chat, then measure behavioral fidelity, structural fidelity, author effort, and human understanding.

### 3. Repository precedent is not retained design intent

Zhu et al., [arXiv:2610.38244](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38244), submitted **28 October 2026**. Inspected primary metadata and selected full-text sections on experiment design, results, open-source validation, discussion, and threats.

**Implication:** coding agents overwhelmingly follow local precedent when it conflicts with their defaults, while explicit priority rules can redirect them. PostCode should keep observed implementation patterns separate from recorded rationale, priority, scope, and current authorization. This supports a bounded evaluation of whether a qualified architectural decision remains effective when nearby implementation precedent points elsewhere. The selected tasks and repositories do not establish maintainability, architecture quality, or human understanding.

### 4. Supervisory observability needs claims, proof, lineage, and unknowns

Khan and Kumar, [arXiv:2610.39468](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39468), submitted **29 October 2026**. Inspected metadata and selected primary full text on positioning, schema, ledger and provenance graph, measurements, views, governance proposal, and validity threats.

**Implication:** Soei is direct prior art for separating an agent's completion claim from supporting evidence and for presenting inferred lineage and unavailable evidence explicitly. Its corpus supplies a useful failure sample for PostCode supervision and hand-back views. The work observes agent activity rather than software architecture, uses one developer's sessions and one harness, and does not evaluate human decision quality; those boundaries should remain visible in any reuse or comparison.

### 5. Qualified architectural enforcement must preserve unknowns

Zhang, [arXiv:2610.41276](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41276), submitted **30 October 2026**. Inspected metadata and selected full text on the decision contract, controlled scenarios, diagnostics, provenance, human-review boundary, discussion, and threats.

**Implication:** the studied architectural-debt gate separates observation qualification, policy identity, inherited debt, and authorized baseline changes, leaving 74 of 240 fixed files unknown rather than manufacturing closure. This is a concrete comparison for PostCode's epistemological contract: an architecture view should expose what was observed, what policy applies, what debt is tolerated, who may change the baseline, and which relations remain unknown. Agreement with one reference is not semantic truth or a language-wide guarantee.

### 6. Plausible explanations do not establish durable understanding

Tavantzis et al., [arXiv:2610.37888](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37888), submitted **27 October 2026**. Inspected metadata and selected full-text sections on the grey-literature method, findings, implications, and validity threats.

**Implication:** the 76-source synthesis frames “cognitive debt” as working software outpacing the understanding retained by people and distinguishes lost rationale from incomplete mental models. For PostCode, this is motivation to evaluate whether a representation supports later explanation, modification, and supervision rather than whether it looks helpful at first exposure. The evidence consists of practitioner accounts, terminology varies, and proposed mitigations have not been evaluated; it is not evidence that PostCode's approach works or that findings transfer to non-programmers.

## Other screened leads

- **What Carries Architectural Signal?**, [arXiv:2610.41628](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41628), and **ASNER**, [arXiv:2610.36894](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36894), provide concrete architecture-mapping and recovery techniques. They remain closer-investigation and possible infrastructure-reuse leads: both predict against reference decompositions, neither evaluates a human-facing architecture surface, and strong agreement can reflect directory organization or one benchmark architecture rather than recovered intent.
- **Beyond Correctness**, [arXiv:2610.32118v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32118), reports that first-year students' declared interventions more often concerned engineering fitness than explicit correctness. This is useful for task design, but the structured self-reports, novice-student setting, and absence of a source-independent architectural interface do not yet add a distinct bibliography comparison.
- **Knowing When to Stop**, [arXiv:2610.31895v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31895), measures over-persistence after evidence of an external blocker and shows that useful escalation reports need progress and verification status. This overlaps the evidence and hand-back concerns already represented by AgentMonBench, AuditKIT, and Soei; retained as an evaluation-scenario lead.
- **Mind the Intent Gap**, [arXiv:2610.37340](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37340), proposes an intent-first industrial workflow and knowledge base. Current evidence is early internal use and subjective reception; the planned controlled evaluation has not occurred, so it remains a provisional process comparison.
- **Cognitive Inclusivity in AI Coding Assistants**, [arXiv:2610.41192](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41192), identifies explanation, validation, learning, feature-discovery, and problem-solving barriers across cognitive styles in a 20-student Copilot study. It may inform later inclusive design but does not yet test architectural practice or the no-source premise.
- **Agent or Human?**, [arXiv:2610.36229](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36229), shows that signature-free commit detection cannot replace recorded attribution. PR #6 already proposes a stronger provenance distinction among account, acting process, human authority, evidence, and exact revision, so no duplicate entry is proposed.
- **Detecting Incorrect LLM Explanations for Automated Program Repair**, [arXiv:2610.38311](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38311), offers a useful adversarial check for generated repair explanations but remains an automated detector with moderate reported performance rather than evidence of a human supervisory interface.
- **Toward Evidence-Based Agentic Software Engineering**, [arXiv:2610.35348](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35348), documents model–harness confounding and weak version/trace reporting across published experiments. Its reporting advice is relevant to later PostCode experiments, but it does not currently change a specific interface or architectural comparison.
