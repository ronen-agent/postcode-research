## Findings and recommended follow-up

### 1. Performance claims need evidence scoped to the claimed effect

**Qi et al., _Merged, Not Measured_**, arXiv:2609.37985, preprint submitted **29 September 2026**. [Metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37985) · [Manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37985v1)

The new bibliography entry records the citation and bounded findings. Inspected sections 3.5.1, 7.1–7.2, and 9 in addition to metadata and introductory material. The study's selected re-executions provide a concrete route to cases where acceptance, test evidence, and intended improvement disagree; they do not estimate a general failure rate.

**PostCode interpretation:** relevant to [research questions](../../research.md#5-research-questions) concerning tests and change supervision, and the [epistemological contract](../../product-design.md#epistemological-contract). Before adopting cases, inspect artifacts and independently reproduce a small selection with clearly stated workloads. Reuse here means evaluation material, not an analysis engine or interface comparator. No experiment was reproduced in this run.

### 2. Reported validation is different from observed validation

**Peng, Calvo, Kalu, and Davis, _How Do Coding Agents Optimize Software and Report Performance Validation?_**, arXiv:2610.03969, preprint submitted **2 October 2026**. [Metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03969) · [Manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03969v1)

Inspected abstract, reported-evidence/metric discussion, and section 5 validity threats. The distinction between documented validation and verified execution is central to interpreting the study. Its sample and methods differ from Qi et al.; their statistics should not be pooled.

**PostCode interpretation:** this sharpens a concrete comparison dimension already in the design: whether a view preserves the boundary between an assertion about a benchmark and an observed run. Examine its evidence taxonomy for candidate test-projection cases; it does not demonstrate a useful human interface.

Obtain the paper and rubrics before deciding whether short explanation probes belong in a future study. Any such addition would be a methodological intervention, not an automatic change made by this watch.
