## Consequences for positioning and next investigations

The recent findings remain useful; the reframing changes their roles more than their factual summaries. Lack of a human interface is not sufficient reason to dismiss research about architectural-account quality or accounts used by another agent. Conversely, successful agent use cannot establish human judgment or collaborative design quality.

Multiple perspectives, maintained descriptions and explicit architectural reasoning already have substantial precedents. PostCode should compare specific mechanisms and tasks: how qualified accounts are obtained, challenged and reused; what happens when perspectives disagree; and whether their use improves a consequential investigation or decision. The additions do not establish that PostCode is novel, unnecessary, or effective.

Three bounded follow-ups would be useful, without becoming roadmap commitments:

1. **Account-quality comparison:** select a real architectural question with a consequential exception to the obvious repository structure. Compare a simple baseline, capable general-purpose investigation and a specialized method; independently assess factual claims, omissions and qualifications.
2. **Retained-account comparison:** revisit one design concern from a different perspective after a change. Examine whether shared accounts preserve intent, expose contradictions and support correction rather than merely reproduce earlier prose.
3. **Decision comparison:** use a concrete alternative and quality-attribute scenario to examine which trade-offs and unresolved evidence participants notice. Assess the resulting decision separately from account quality or participant satisfaction.

For each, keep human source exposure, human experience, agent source access, available tools and retained context explicit. A design agent using PostCode is a valid configuration; PostCode need not host the conversation.
