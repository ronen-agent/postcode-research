# Formative Evidence Candidates

Status: optional instrumentation and observation ideas; not a mandatory event schema

The governing minimum for research episodes appears in [`methodology.md`](../methodology.md#formative-evidence-capture). This note preserves additional evidence that may be useful when a particular research question warrants the cost and complexity of collecting it.

Automatic capture is valuable when retrospective reconstruction would be unreliable. It does not by itself create an unbiased dataset, explain activity outside PostCode, or establish that a result was correct or useful.

## Episode and provenance context

- current task and information need;
- PostCode version, configuration, and research-session identifier;
- repository, revision, branch, or relevant working-tree state;
- references to external tasks, agent conversations, or design artifacts;
- whether the episode concerns inspection, development, design, or implementation review.

## Requests and initiation

- prompts and prose requests submitted to PostCode;
- whether activity was initiated by a human, external agent, or PostCode;
- for PostCode-initiated investigation, whether it was request-scoped or autonomous and the parent request or trigger;
- explicit and automatically selected lenses, parameters, presentations, and alternatives considered;
- suggestions accepted, rejected, ignored, or deferred.

## Results and interaction

- projections and views produced, opened, closed, revisited, or exported;
- captured bases, evidence, provenance, guarantees, limitations, and unavailable results;
- composition of projections within views;
- entities and relationships discovered or pursued;
- navigation and derivation lineage among views;
- changes in captured basis;
- comparisons among implemented, hypothetical, and intended material;
- source escape-hatch use;
- agent access to shared context and agent-requested investigation;
- applicability, availability, execution state, materialization, cost estimates, resource choices, and actual cost where relevant.

## Contemporaneous reports

- wanted information or presentation that was unavailable;
- distrust, confusion, loss of orientation, or perceived inadequacy;
- a view considered useful, surprising, misleading, or unnecessarily prominent;
- inability to express an intended change using available concepts and context;
- optional explanation of a positive or negative immediate reaction;
- relevant activity outside PostCode reported voluntarily by the participant.

## Subsequent activity

- further views, subjects, or questions pursued;
- request for more detail or a different representation;
- return to ordinary chat, source, or another tool;
- abandonment or deferral of the investigation;
- design or implementation action influenced by the result;
- later correction, extension, contradiction, or reuse of retained understanding.

## Interpretation cautions

- Absence of an inadequacy report does not establish adequacy.
- Continued use does not establish preference or effectiveness when the investigator is deliberately tolerating friction.
- A recorded reaction does not establish factual account quality.
- A successful task does not establish that the participant understood the architecture.
- Instrumentation can change behavior and should remain proportionate to the question.
- Additional fields should be removed or ignored when they do not contribute to a plausible analysis.
